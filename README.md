# soundAS — web build

This repository holds **only the compiled web build** of soundAS. It is
written by CI and force-pushed on every release; nothing here is edited
by hand, and the history is deliberately shallow.

The app itself: **https://soundas.ir** — also served, unchanged, from
**https://amirsinam.github.io/soundas/**. The two addresses hold separate
libraries: a browser stores your songs per address, so songs added at one
are not visible at the other.

## What soundAS is

A player for the songs you already own. You bring your own files and it
plays and organises them. Nothing is streamed, nothing is downloaded
from anywhere, and no audio is ever shared between users. In a browser
your songs stay on your own device — they are never uploaded.
